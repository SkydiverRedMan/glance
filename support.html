My events aren't showing

Open Settings, tap Apps, then Glance, and make sure Calendars is set to Full Access. Then tap Calendars in Glance and check the calendar is ticked.

The Lock Screen countdown didn't start

iOS only lets Glance start a countdown while it's open, so open Glance within 3 hours of your event. "Next Event on Lock Screen" must be on in the Calendars screen.

A countdown says Ended

Tap Clear, or open Glance. iOS also removes it when it next wakes Glance, which can take a while.

My alerts

Alerts are saved with the event in your calendar and sent by iOS. If one doesn't arrive, check that notifications are on for Apple's Calendar in the Settings app.
