Glance

Privacy Policy

Glance shows the calendars already on your iPhone.

What Glance reads

With your permission, Glance reads and changes the events in your iPhone's calendars, using Apple's calendar system. It only does this to show them to you and to make the changes you ask for.

Where your data goes

Glance doesn't collect your data. It has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads, and it doesn't send your data to the developer or to anyone else. If you sync your calendars through an account such as iCloud or Google, that syncing is done by iOS and that provider's terms apply.

Directions

If you ask for directions, Glance hands that event's location to the maps app you choose, and that app's own privacy policy applies. Glance doesn't use your location.

Saved settings

Your display choices, such as which calendars are hidden and which day starts the week, are saved on your iPhone only. Deleting Glance removes them. Your events stay in your calendars.

Changing your mind

You can turn off calendar access at any time in the Settings app, under Glance.
